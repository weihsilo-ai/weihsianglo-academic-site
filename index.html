About

I am a Human Factors researcher and Ph.D. student in Industrial and Operations Engineering at the MUniversity of Michigan, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Intelligence & Human Augmentation Lab (iHub) and am advised by Dr. Manhua Wang.

I hold an M.S. in Human Factors and Ergonomics from San José State University, where I previously conducted research at the Behavior, Accessibility, and Technology Lab (BAT Lab) and was advised by Dr. Gaojian Huang.

Research interests

  • Human behavior modeling grounded in human factors theories and frameworks.
  • Risk perception, situation awareness, and decision-making during human-machine interaction.
  • Behavioral and multimodal datasets used to develop theory-based quantitative models for actionable feedback and system interventions in automated driving and intelligent mobility.
